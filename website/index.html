UNBREAKABLE SAFETY

The Anti-Ban Physics Architecture

How Twixor stays 100% indistinguishable from human browsing behavior.

🔥

Cubic Bézier Cursor Flights

Mouse movements never follow straight computer vectors. Twixor computes dynamic cubic Bézier trajectories with realistic micro-tremors (1-3px variance) simulating authentic human hand physics.

⌨

Human Typing & Typos

Text is typed character-by-character using synthetic pointer events (35ms–110ms variance). Extended thinking pauses occur at punctuation marks, with occasional simulated typos and backspacing.

🛡

Virtualized DOM Scroll Guard

Twitter virtualizes feeds. Twixor uses chunked micro-flick scrolls with hydration resting pauses. When reply modals or compose sheets open, background scrolling freezes completely to prevent disorienting jumps.

🚫

Direct Discard Protocol

When skipping a tweet, Twitter often prompts "Save post to drafts?". Twixor instantly detects and clicks Discard using genuine pointer events, neutralizing infinite modal traps and reload loops.

⏱

Central Safety Pacing Delays

Every agent strictly obeys user-configured profile waits (e.g. 0-5s), reply delays (e.g. 1-4s), and automatic batch resting breaks (e.g. 2-min pause every 15 engagements).

🔒

Strict Account Lock

The extension verifies that the active Twitter account matches your authorized handle before performing any engagement, safeguarding your brand from accidental cross-account actions.
